Educational app only

Boussole helps beginners understand investment risk with real market data and simulations. It is not a bank, a broker or a financial adviser, it gives no investment advice, and no real transaction is possible.

About

Boussole is a school project, carried out in the final year of the Engineering degree in Computer Science and Cybersecurity at INSA Hauts-de-France.

This app reflects what its developer understood. It is based on his personal experience and research, and may contain simplifications or mistakes. Please keep this in mind when using it.
