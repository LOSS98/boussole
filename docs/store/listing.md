## English
**Name:** Boussole: Learn Risk
**Subtitle (iOS, 30):** Understand investment risk
**Short description (Play, 80):** Learn to read investment risk with real market data and fun simulations.
**Full description:**
Boussole teaches beginners to read the RISK of an investment, not just its return.

• Catalog of 30 assets (stocks, ETFs, crypto) with real historical data
• Risk indicators explained simply: volatility, maximum drawdown, recovery time, probability of loss
• Risk clock: see how the holding period changes your chances of losing money
• Panic button: replay real crashes (2000, 2008, 2020, 2022, 2025) and test your reactions with virtual money
• Quizzes and a daily streak to learn step by step
• Glossary, favourites, saved simulations, offline mode

No account. No ads. No tracking. No real money.

Boussole is educational only. It is not a bank, broker or financial adviser and gives no investment advice. Past performance does not predict future results.
